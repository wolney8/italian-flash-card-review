What “most consonants” means

Ordinary masculine openings use i / dei

It means a masculine noun beginning with a normal consonant sound that is not in the special group.

i bambini / dei bambini
the children / some children
i cani / dei cani
the dogs / some dogs
i gatti / dei gatti
the cats / some cats
i libri / dei libri
the books / some books
i maestri / dei maestri
the teachers / some teachers
i problemi / dei problemi
the problems / some problems
Important: the letter p followed by another consonant is not automatically special. Say i problemi / dei problemi and i professori / dei professori. A genuinely special start such as ps- uses gli / degli: gli psicologi / degli psicologi.

Decision path

Choose the article in three steps

1. Do you mean the or some?
2. Is the plural noun feminine? Use le or delle.
3. If it is masculine, inspect the beginning: ordinary consonant → i / dei; vowel or special opening → gli / degli.
