Date: 24 November 2026

## Purpose

Expand the existing article practice with a complete, regularly accessible reference and a focused daily exercise. The page explains how meaning, noun gender, number and starting sound determine the article.

## Reference content

Five noun patterns cover the full common article matrix: masculine ordinary consonant, masculine special start, masculine vowel, feminine consonant and feminine vowel.

The guide includes:

- Definite singular and plural articles for **the**.
- Singular indefinite articles for **a/an**.
- Partitive singular amounts and plural countable things for **some**.
- All seven **di + definite article** combinations.
- An explicit **s + consonant** and **s + vowel** comparison.
- The feminine contrast **la scuola / una scuola**.
- Apostrophes, **un amico / un’amica**, and **gli amici / le amiche**.
- Expandable notes for **gn, ps, x, consonantal i/y, pn** and silent **h**.
- A distinction between partitive **some** and prepositional **of the**.

## Exercise bank

80 exercises: 24 definite, 16 indefinite, 24 partitive and 16 rule questions.

Each daily round contains ten distinct questions. Learners can select a mixed round or a single family. Mixed rounds include article choice, typed gaps, singular-to-plural changes, contraction formation and rule recognition. Another round changes the question selection.

Instructions are in English. The Italian prompt and English meaning occupy separate lines. Optional hints give gender, number and starting-letter guidance. Answers and explanations appear after an attempt. Missed questions can be practised again.

Progress and draft answers use the new browser key `italianArticleGuideV1`. Existing practice state and Anki content are separate.
