## Useful classroom questions and memory aids

**Lesson 6 slides11–12, minutes pp4–5, tables p3; repeated Lesson 7 slides7–8:**

- Question models: `Chi sei?`, `Come stai?`, `Dove sei?`, `Di dove sei?`, `Che cosa è / Cos’è?`, `Quando vieni?`, `Perché vieni?`.
- Meaning: `Che cosa significa “letto”?` / `“Letto” significa “bed”.`
- Spelling: `Come si scrive “letto” in italiano?` / `Si scrive ELLE – E – TI – TI – O.`
- Saying it: `Come si dice “bed” in italiano?` / `Si dice “letto”.`
- Repair: `Non ho capito!`
- Informal repetition: `Come, scusa? Puoi ripetere, per favore?`
- Formal repetition: `Come, scusi? Può ripetere, per favore?`

Other explicit aids: formal `Lei` uses third-person singular (L1 p2); `è` versus `e`; `sono` as both “I am” and “they are”; adjective endings (`Sono italiano/italiana`); `non` before the verb; question intonation illustrated by `Claudia è di Vienna.` / `Claudia è di Vienna?`; regular -ARE stem plus endings; teaching stress marks `Lavoràre`, `Lavoriàmo`, `Lavoràte`, `Lavòro`, `Lavòri`, `Lavòra`, `Lavòrano` (L7 slide13). The latter accents are teaching marks, not ordinary spelling requirements.

## Future source ingestion

1. **Preserve identity.** Retain original filename, extension, byte count, SHA-256 and provider identity. Assign an immutable source ID. Append new IDs without renumbering existing source, phrase, use or homework records.
2. **Separate formats.** A PDF and PPTX with the same filename stem need distinct extraction records. Compare hashes before merging duplicate uploads.
3. **Keep reliable locators.** Record 1-based file pages or slide numbers. Distinguish printed textbook pages from PDF pages. Use DOCX body-block references until pagination has been rendered and checked.
4. **Read tables and notes.** Preserve table cells and complete text runs. Inspect speaker-note XML for additional teaching content. A slide’s visible text is not the whole presentation source.
5. **Inspect teaching images.** Visually review scanned pages, image tables and embedded figures. Record exact text, visible attribution and uncertain readings. A blank native-text extract does not prove a blank page.
6. **Preserve source variants.** Keep original wording and source typos in the ledger. Record teaching corrections and adaptations separately.
7. **Separate assignments.** Distinguish homework, classwork and optional revision. Keep exact page/exercise references. Missing pages or audio remain explicit gaps.
8. **Label adaptations honestly.** A source-labelled verbatim phrase must match its cited source. Changed conjugations, new subjects or new examples are adapted or supplementary material.
9. **Verify later lessons.** Will confirmed `prendere` on 25 November 2026. Preserve that learner confirmation while keeping Laura’s exact Lesson 9 categories, examples and homework pending until the originals arrive.
10. **Keep counts meaningful.** Source inventory, implemented practice, validation and publication are separate states. Update each only with corresponding evidence.
